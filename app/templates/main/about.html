Who We Are

About The Church

PCEA Ngecha Mother Church exists to glorify God through worship, discipleship, fellowship, and mission. We are a covenant family rooted in Scripture, prayer, and compassionate service to our community.

This platform serves as our digital home for sharing ministry updates, equipping believers, and helping members stay connected throughout the week.

Vision and Mission

  • Preach Christ faithfully through teaching and worship.
  • Disciple children, youth, and adults through intentional learning pathways.
  • Serve the community through care, outreach, and holistic development.
  • Raise leaders who model integrity, humility, and Christ-like character.

What You Can Explore Here

  • Weekly sermons, devotion moments, and event schedules
  • District profiles, church groups, and leadership team details
  • Prayer support, contact channels, and newsletter updates

Our Worship Rhythm

We gather each week for corporate worship, intercession, and biblical teaching, then continue ministry in homes, districts, and fellowship spaces throughout the week.

Whether you are a long-time member or visiting for the first time, you are warmly invited to grow in faith and community with us.
